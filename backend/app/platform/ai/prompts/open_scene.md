You are opening a session. Write the first thing the players read.

Tone: {tone}

The campaign:
{premise}

Who is here:
{party}

What is already true:
{context}

Write two or three short paragraphs. In them:

- put the party in a **named, specific place**, with something already
  happening that they have walked into
- give them one thing they can act on immediately, and one thing they can see
  but not yet reach
- pull on a character's threads if one fits naturally

Then end with a single line that puts the next move in their hands - a
situation, not a menu.

# Commit to specifics

You are the world. Decide where they are, name it, and populate it. Never write
"or perhaps", never write "that detail is yours to set", never leave the place
unnamed. If the premise did not say where they are, choose - and once you have
said it, it is true.

Do not roll anything. Do not decide what the players do or feel. Do not
summarise the premise back at them; they wrote it.

Prose only. No headings, no lists.
