You are opening a session. Write the first thing the players read.

Tone: {tone}

The campaign:
{premise}

Who is here:
{party}

What is already true:
{context}

Write two or three short paragraphs. In them:

- put the party somewhere specific, with something happening
- give them at least one thing they can act on immediately, and one thing they
  can see but not yet reach
- pull on something from a character's threads if one fits naturally

Then end with a single line, on its own, that asks what they do. Not a menu of
options - a situation that makes the next move obvious enough to attempt and
open enough to surprise you.

Do not roll anything. Do not decide what the players do or feel. Do not
summarise the premise back at them - they wrote it.

Prose only. No headings, no lists.
